## Cross-cutting prohibitions (apply to every row)
- **The app never holds worker money.** Holding it would be an unregulated deposit (2-7 years' jail) [REG-081] and breaks the rule that MF payments come from the investor's own account [REG-066].
- **No lucky draws or prize-linked saving** run by a non-bank [REG-079]. **No pooled "savings circles"** without chit registration in each state [REG-080].
- **No cashback on MF or insurance purchases** [REG-082, 083].
- **Data.** Until 13 May 2027 the IT Act s.43A, the SPDI Rules and s.72A apply. From then the full DPDP Act applies, with penalties up to Rs 250 crore [REG-084, 085]. The **Bueno/BetterPlace borrower list cannot be reused** without the data owner acting and fresh consent [REG-086].
- **Labour Codes.** Aggregators pay 1-2% of turnover (capped at 5% of worker payouts) into a *government* fund. The rate is not yet notified. There is no private-product hook [REG-062, 063]. Delhi has no gig-worker Act [REG-065].
