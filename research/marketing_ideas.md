# Marketing: eight ways to reach gig and blue-collar savers cheaply

As of 30 Sep 2026. CAC is **fully loaded, per activated saver** (first successful auto-debit), from `model/savings_first_model.xlsx` → *Funnel & CAC*. CAC figures are shown as base (bear–bull). Every channel assumption is an **assumption**: no Indian savings app publishes its CAC [BM-04, BM-05]. The nearest founder anchor is Bueno's cost per loan applicant, under ₹150. Legal points cite rows in `regulation_rails.csv` (REG-) and `own_checks.csv` (OWN-).

**What the law rules out everywhere:**
- no cashback, gifts or cash bonuses for investing in a fund [REG-082];
- no insurance rebates [REG-083];
- no lucky draws [REG-079];
- no pooled savings circles [REG-080];
- the app never holds the worker's money [REG-081].

So every idea below grows through convenience, habit and trust, not prizes.

| # | Idea | How it works | CAC per activated saver | What it needs | Legal check |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Save on payout day, through the platform** ★ | The platform shows a "save part of today's payout" prompt in its partner app. The worker sets a UPI mandate that debits their own bank right after each payout lands. | **₹220** (₹197–254). Field staff at hubs ₹112 + KYC ₹52 + mandate ₹6 + assumed partner fee ₹50. Converts best (54% of installs activate, assumed) | A platform partner (Swiggy relationship from Bueno); a payment aggregator | A platform cannot pay into a worker's fund; the payout goes to the worker's bank first, then the worker's own mandate [REG-066]. No rule found on payout deductions [REG-067]. Platform co-funding of savings is a platform benefit (tax and labour treatment unverified). Karnataka's welfare fee can't be passed to workers [REG-064]. |
| 2 | **Onboarding at rider hubs, dark stores and driver stands** ★ | Field agents with QR codes do assisted sign-up in 10 minutes during shift gaps. | **₹213** (₹185–255). Agent ₹25k a month ÷ 25 days ÷ 20 installs = ₹50 per install; 36% activate | Hub access (platform or fleet permission); a field team | Anyone who explains or sells funds needs NISM V-A certification and an EUIN [REG-036, REG-037]. No gifts at sign-up [REG-082]. |
| 3 | **Savings buddy** ★ | Two friends set a shared goal and see each other's streaks. Each saves into their own account; nothing is pooled. | **₹73** in all cases (KYC + mandate only; no bonus) | Product feature only | **No cash referral bonus** for fund saving [REG-082]. No pooling, which would be a chit fund needing state registration [REG-080]. |
| 4 | **Local-language creators and WhatsApp groups** | Hindi, Bhojpuri and similar creators make education-only videos. WhatsApp communities of riders get utility messages. | **₹298** (₹256–360). 19% of installs activate | Creator partnerships; WhatsApp Business (marketing message ₹0.86) [REG-072] | Creators must not recommend funds or claim returns, and must not be paid as unregistered advisers [OWN-03]. |
| 5 | **Festival and goal campaigns** (Diwali, school fees, bike down payment) | A goal FD that matures before the date; campaigns time the push to payday before the festival. | Lowers CAC on existing channels; not modelled as a channel | Partner bank with goal-dated FDs | No lucky draws or "save and win" [REG-079]. The FD is in the worker's own name [REG-081]. |
| 6 | **Fleet owners and staffing firms** | EV-fleet and cab-fleet operators, and staffing firms for warehouse and security staff, offer savings at induction. | Lever D (blue-collar): being researched | Employer or fleet partners | Payroll deduction into funds is only in a SEBI **draft** (May 2026), limited to listed or EPFO-registered employers [REG-066]. **Bueno's employer channel failed ("TAM was small")**, so treat it as a second channel, not the engine. |
| 7 | **Women-first** | Campaigns and a product for women workers. | Not modelled | A different segment | **Women are under 1% of delivery riders** [INC-01], so this doesn't fit the first segment. It fits domestic, care and beauty workers later. |
| 8 | **Cash top-ups at kiranas** | Workers paid in cash deposit at a nearby shop. | Not modelled | A **bank business-correspondent** network | The app can never touch cash or hold money [REG-081]. Only a bank's business correspondent can take the deposit, into the worker's own account. **Drop, or partner a bank's BC network.** |

★ = the three to feature in the deck. They have the lowest CAC, need no licence beyond fund distribution, and survive the legal checks. **Paid ads are the benchmark to beat at ₹750 (₹621–944).** Save-at-source costs less than a third of that.

**The channel to drop from the brief:** reviving old Bueno borrowers. That list belongs to BetterPlace and was collected for lending, so contacting those people for a savings app breaks purpose limits under today's IT Act rules and the DPDP Act from 13 May 2027 [REG-084–086].

## Added 30 Sep 2026: the self-employed half (half of all savers)

The founder asked for half of all savers to come from shopkeepers, self-employed tradespeople, drivers outside the apps and small business owners. They are offered the same products as gig workers. CAC is the model's base case per first debit, and the base case adds half again to the acquisition part for this half, because no payments firm publishes its merchant acquisition cost [NG-043].

| # | Idea | How it works | CAC | Legal check |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | **Trade associations, cooperatives, self-help groups** ★ | Group sessions; each member saves into their own account | **₹185** | No pooling, which would be a chit fund [REG-080]. No fee to the association for fund sales (AMFI: commission only to ARN holders) [OWN-05]. No fee for insurance leads (Insurance Act s.40) [OWN-06] |
| 10 | **Save from daily UPI sales** ★ | A mandate timed after the shop's daily UPI collections land | **₹243** | Small merchants' UPI money lands straight in their own account [NG-030]. Fund purchases must come from the saver's own account [REG-066]. The payment partner's fee is a flat service fee, never a commission on fund or insurance sales (legal opinion needed) [OWN-07]. No such product exists today [NG-046] |
| 11 | **Supplier and distributor networks** ★ | FMCG or wholesale distributors who visit shops weekly introduce us | **₹274** | May be paid only for gold referrals, unless they register as ARN sub-distributors with NISM-certified staff [OWN-05]. Never for insurance [OWN-06]. The supplier finance that exists today (udaan, HUL) is trade credit, which this plan does not offer [NG-044] |
| 12 | **Field agents in markets** | Agents walk market streets and sign shops up | **₹302** | NISM certification for anyone who explains funds; no sign-up gifts [OWN-05, REG-082] |

★ = on slide 10 of the deck, alongside the three gig channels.
