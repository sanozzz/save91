A6

The pattern: savings and wallets reach people cheaply, but money comes from credit or from the flow of payments. Every savings-first player that lasted added lending. Nubank's card-first path does not transfer: in India only banks and RBI-approved NBFCs can issue cards, and thin-file workers are best reached with an FD-secured card. On whether saving predicts repayment, the best evidence is correlation only: savings-linked self-help-group loans run an NPA of 2.05% vs 8.72% for microfinance.
