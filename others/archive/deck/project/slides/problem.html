The problem

With no savings buffer, one bad week pushes workers into costly debt, and no formal product fits daily income.

1. A slow week or a breakdown cuts earnings

2. No savings to fall back on

3. Borrow fast: loan app, moneylender, platform advance

Say: The loop on the left is what a rider's month looks like with no buffer. One slow week or a breakdown cuts earnings; with nothing saved they borrow fast from a loan app, a moneylender or a platform advance; repayments then eat next week's earnings. On the right, the evidence: 40% of gig workers earn under ₹15,000 a month; small personal loans of ₹10-50k reach 90+ days late at 6-7%; and formal saving doesn't fit daily cash. Most funds need ₹100 for a daily SIP, and SEBI's small-SIP scheme is monthly only. Likely question: Isn't this what microfinance solves? Answer: Microfinance lends. It doesn't build a buffer, and RBI now caps a low-income household's repayments at half its income.
