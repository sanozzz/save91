Appendix A9

The hardest VC questions, with answers (3 of 3).

Why you?

I ran Bueno: 15,000+ loans a month to this segment. Before that, credit policy at Capital One, loss models for Citi at EXL, Encore Capital, and analytics at Swiggy.

Why did Bueno end?

BetterPlace acquired it in May 2023. The lessons: first loans lost money, platforms were the best channel, and the employer channel was too small.

What does the loss cover cost?

About 5% of each covered loan held as cash: ₹0.09 per ₹1 of year-1 revenue. Lenders then pay about 1.5% + 5.5% of each loan, not 1.5%.

Why two customer groups from day one?

The self-employed save more and stay longer, lifting the best case from 4.1 to 5.3 times CAC. The cost: two sales approaches and riskier borrowers. The pilot reads each half separately.

Why would a platform partner with you?

A free savings benefit for riders as the Labour Codes push social security, and Bueno's Swiggy relationship. Unproven: the pilot needs one platform to sign.
