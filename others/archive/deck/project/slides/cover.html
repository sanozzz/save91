An honest test · 30 September 2026

Save first, lend later

Gig workers and the self-employed could save ₹40–₹50 a day automatically, and that habit might be the cheapest way to lend to them later.

This deck tests whether the numbers add up. Short answer: not in the base case. In the best case, yes, and cross-sell is the one thing that must go right.

Sandeep Arora · co-founder and CEO of Bueno Finance 2019–22 (YC W21) · IIT Delhi

Say: This is a micro-savings app for two kinds of people in equal numbers: app delivery and ride workers, and the self-employed, meaning shopkeepers, tradespeople, drivers outside the apps and small business owners. They save ₹40 to ₹50 a day automatically, the money sits with partners and never with us, and we earn later by offering loans, cards and insurance. I ran the opposite model at Bueno, lending first to gig and blue-collar workers. This deck is an honest test of whether saving first is the better business. The answer: not on today's evidence in the base case, and yes in the best case, where cross-sell is the one assumption that must hold. The last slide is a one-year pilot that settles it. Likely question: So is this a pitch or not? Answer: It is a test. I want you to see the numbers that would make me stop, before any money goes in.
