Say: Bottom-up: savers acquired each year, half gig and half self-employed, times revenue per saver by year of their life, times how many stay. The growth plan is an assumption: 25,000 savers in year 1, rising to 7,000,000 new savers a year by year 10, with CAC rising as channels saturate. In the best case revenue reaches $115M in year 10 from 7.3M active savers, and first passes $100M in year 10; in the base case $17M. With gig workers only, the best case stops at $93M. The pool is not the constraint: India had about 12M gig workers in FY25, heading to 23.5M, and 5.36 crore micro enterprises are registered on Udyam. Dollars at ₹96.1. Likely question: How much capital does the best case need? Answer: It peaks at ₹91 crore; the base case never turns cash-positive, which is the point of the pilot.
