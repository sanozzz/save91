A5b

The legal constraint that shapes these channels: under AMFI rules a fund distributor may share commission only with sub-distributors who hold their own registration, and under section 40 of the Insurance Act nobody may be paid for insurance leads except a licensed agent or intermediary. So a supplier's salesmen or a trade association can be paid only for gold referrals, which are unregulated, unless they register. Payment aggregators may pay out on a merchant's instruction, but fund purchases must come from the merchant's own bank account, so saving from UPI sales is a mandate timed after the day's collections land. Supplier-network finance that exists today (udaan, HUL) is trade credit for stock, which the plan does not offer.
