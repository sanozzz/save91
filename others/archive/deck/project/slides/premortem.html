Say: Assume it is 2028 and this failed. The likeliest reasons, each with the signal we would see first. Savers stop: if under 50% are still saving at month 3, we are below even the base case. Fees stay thin: our base assumes lenders pay about 7% of each loan with our 5% cover; Moneyview's partner fees are about about 11% of partner loans, but it lends to people earning far more. Gold rules tighten: the Finance Ministry is reportedly consulting on making digital gold a security. Losses beat the cover: our base assumes 4.5% of gig first loans and 7% of self-employed first loans go 90+ days late, against 6-7% for small personal loans and 10.2% for small business loans to the self-employed; PM SVANidhi street-vendor loans ran 12–16% bad, and Delhi microfinance has 21.3% of its book 180+ days late. Moneyview actually paid 3.4% of covered loans in FY26. Two customers also means two sales approaches for a small team: if the halves' readings diverge by month 6, the money goes to the one that works. Debits: the list price is ₹5 a debit. And a big app could do this: PhonePe already offers daily gold with Jar. Likely question: Which of these worries you most? Answer: Retention. Riders change platforms about every 13 months, and any UPI app can now move their mandate.
