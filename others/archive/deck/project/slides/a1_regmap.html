A1

The distribution model needs an AMFI registration, IRDAI corporate-agent status and contracts with lenders, a gold vault partner and a bank. Only lever B, an own NBFC, needs real capital: ₹10 crore minimum, and in practice equity near a third of the loan book. The FD-backed card must come from the same bank that holds the FD.
