Save first, lend later

Worth testing, not yet building: as a savings-led lender to shopkeepers, it pays back in about two-thirds of futures.

Every assumption checked against outside evidence. One point and one picture per slide; the numbers sit in the appendix.

This note asks one question: should we build a save-first, lend-later business? My answer: worth testing, not yet building. As a savings-led lender to shopkeepers, a customer pays back in about two-thirds of the futures we simulated. Four numbers decide it: what it costs to win a shopkeeper, how many borrow, the rate we can charge, and how well they repay. One pilot can measure all four before we spend real money. I'd value your honest view; the last slide has three questions.
