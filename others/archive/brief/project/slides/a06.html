The funnel explains the economics. Of 100 shopkeepers we win, about 39 are still saving at six months and 24 at a year, which is what Indian small-saver evidence suggests. If 1 in 5 proven savers borrow each year, about 23 loans follow over five years; if 35% do, 41. Each loan has to pay for the cost of winning and serving several customers who never borrow.
