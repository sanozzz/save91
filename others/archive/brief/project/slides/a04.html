Each loan is repaid in equal monthly instalments. Shopkeepers: ₹1.5 lakh over 12 months at 26% a year; drivers: ₹1 lakh over 18 months at 26%; riders: ₹25,000 over 6 months at 30%; processing fee 2.5-3%. We pay 13–15% to borrow. Bad-loan losses and running costs are shares of the loan balance a year: 3% and 3% if savers repay better; 6% and 5% normal; 9% and 8% as digital lenders report today. Winning the customer is counted separately.
