Who to serve follows from the loan they can carry. Shopkeepers take ₹1.5 lakh loans and pay back in 64 in 100 futures: start there. Drivers and home-service workers need ₹1 lakh vehicle and tool loans and pay back in 50 in 100; they come next, through platforms that already deduct repayments from payouts. Delivery riders borrow about ₹25,000 at a time, which never covers the cost of winning them, so they can save but will not pay. Salaried workers through employers pay back in 43 in 100: they stay longer but can borrow more cheaply elsewhere, so they pay only at rates of 26% or more, or if taking repayment from pay keeps losses low (appendix).
