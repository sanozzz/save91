5 / 10How the model works

So we build the business around the visit: sign up, visit, move to UPI, lend, and collect on the same visit.

One person, one relationship: the agent who keeps him saving is the one who collects his loan.
The cash rules shape the loop: fund money must come from the saver's own bank account (GIG-32); cash into funds is capped at ₹50,000 per fund house a year.

GST on our fees makes it worse
If the lender's 1% includes 18% GST, we keep ₹2,119 of each ₹2,500. Check the contract.
Costs beyond the agent makes it worse
App, KYC (₹3 per Aadhaar e-KYC, REG-068), support and cash banking are not counted.
Cash rules for funds makes it worse
Fund money must come from the saver's own bank account (GIG-32); cash into funds is capped at ₹50,000 per fund house a year.
Agent ramp-up and churn makes it worse
Full output from month 1 is assumed. A new agent, or one who leaves, earns less.
Copying risk
PhonePe already offers a ₹100-a-day deposit (since 6 Aug 2026). That hits retention.
Credit losses risk
On referred loans the lender carries them, but high losses mean fewer approvals.
Everything left out makes the result worse or riskier, so the middle case is, if anything, generous.
