Lever A, a 5% loss cover, lets lenders pay us about 1.5% plus 5.5% of each loan, against 1-3% for plain sourcing; Moneyview's partner fees are about about 11% of partner loans. Lever B, an own NBFC, pays about 14% to borrow and needs equity of about a third of its book; small digital NBFCs lose money for their first years. Lever C, gold, earns about 1.5% of each purchase. The last row shows what the self-employed half adds: with gig workers only, the plan's best case is 4.1 and $93M in year 10. Salaried blue-collar workers through employers were tested earlier and dropped: flexi staff churn 21-22% a quarter.
