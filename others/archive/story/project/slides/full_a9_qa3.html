Appendix A9

The hardest VC questions, with answers (3 of 3).

Why you?

I have lent to this segment at scale: 15,000+ loans a month. Before that, credit policy at Capital One, loss models for Citi at EXL, Encore Capital, and analytics at Swiggy.

Do most savers ever borrow?

No. In the base case 1 in 16 takes a loan within five years, so each borrower costs ₹4,006 to find, against ₹760 lending first.

What does the loss cover cost?

About 5% of each covered loan held as cash: ₹0.09 per ₹1 of year-1 revenue. Lenders then pay about 1.5% + 5.5% of each loan, not 1.5%.

Why two customer groups from day one?

The self-employed save more and stay longer, lifting the best case from 4.1 to 5.3 times CAC. The cost: two sales approaches and riskier borrowers. The pilot reads each half separately.

Why would a platform partner with you?

A free savings benefit for riders as the Labour Codes push social security, and an existing relationship with Swiggy. Unproven: the pilot needs one platform to sign.
