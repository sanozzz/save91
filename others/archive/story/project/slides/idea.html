The idea

Workers save a little every day; once we see who is steady, partners lend to them and insure them, and we earn a fee.

The problem

A delivery rider or a shopkeeper earns every day but saves nothing, so a bad week means borrowing fast and at a high price.

Our idea

An app that saves ₹40–₹50 a day from their own bank account into gold, a fund or an FD in their own name. That record then earns them loans and cover from partners.

1. Saves ₹40–₹50 a day, automatically

2. The money sits with a bank, fund or gold partner, in the saver's name

3. Months of saving show who is steady

4. Partners lend and insure; we earn a fee on each

What kind of company: a distributor with an app, like an insurance or loan agent. Partners hold the money and the loans; we cover at most 5% of loan losses. Who: half app delivery and ride workers, half shopkeepers and the self-employed.

Say: The problem is simple. People who earn daily, a Swiggy rider or a kirana owner, save nothing, so when a bad week comes they borrow fast from a loan app or a moneylender. Our idea: an app that auto-saves ₹40 to ₹50 a day from their own bank account, through UPI Autopay, into gold, a liquid fund or a bank FD held in their own name. After a few months the saving record shows who is steady, and partners offer them loans and insurance through us. We are a distributor, not a lender: we never hold the money, the partner lender carries the loan, and we cover at most 5% of its losses, the most RBI allows. Likely question: Why would a saver want a loan from us? Answer: They already borrow; we can offer a cheaper loan because we can see their saving.
