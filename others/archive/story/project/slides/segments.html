Say: Half our savers are delivery and ride workers, half are shopkeepers, tradespeople, drivers outside the apps and small business owners. The model assumes the self-employed save ₹50 a day against ₹40 and stay longer, 25% still saving after a year against 20%, but go 90+ days late on 7% of loans against 4.5%. In the middle case both halves lose money. In the best case the self-employed reach 6.6 times their cost against 4.1, which is why the mix makes the best case sturdier: gig workers alone would stop at $93M in year 10. Likely question: Why not start with one group? Answer: Two groups means two sales approaches for a small team. If their month-6 readings diverge, the next year's money goes to the one that works.
