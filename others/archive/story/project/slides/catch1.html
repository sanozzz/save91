Say: This is the first catch, and the old deck never said it plainly. Take one saver in their second year, middle case. They bring us ₹438 a year: gold margin ₹135, loan fees ₹188, insurance commission ₹86. Serving them costs ₹789, and ₹534 of that is debit fees alone: about ₹2 a debit, about 20 debits a month, plus pre-debit notices and failed debits. Support and tech add ₹108. The money itself earns almost nothing: ₹2 from the fund and ₹5 from FDs. In the best case the picture flips: ₹1,865 in, ₹435 out, mostly because a debit costs ₹0.50 and far more savers buy. Likely question: Why not debit weekly? Answer: That cuts the fee by about 80%, but the daily habit is the product. The pilot prices daily debits before launch; if the price stays high, switch to weekly.
