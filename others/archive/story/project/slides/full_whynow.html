Say: Six rule changes set the frame. The doors: RBI's digital lending rules let an app cover up to 5% of a partner's loan losses, which lets us earn more per loan without becoming a lender; and RBI's 2026 e-mandate framework lets auto-debits up to ₹15,000 run without fresh approval. UPI Autopay is the standing instruction that debits a bank account on schedule; it carries no merchant fee even after the new fee on UPI payments above ₹2,000 from 15 Oct 2026. The fences: RBI bars loans against gold, including digital gold, from April 2026; NPCI lets any UPI app show and move a saver's mandate; SEBI says digital gold is outside its rules; and IRDAI's draft of 23 Sep 2026 would cap insurance commissions. The Labour Codes of 21 Nov 2025 cover gig workers but give a private savings product no hook. Likely question: Is 2025-26 a real window? Answer: The doors are real but small. The fences matter more: they decide which savings product we can build on.
