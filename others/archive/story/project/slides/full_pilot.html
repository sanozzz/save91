Say: The next step is not a big round. It is a one-year pilot with 25,000 savers in Delhi NCR: half gig workers through one platform partner, half shopkeepers and the self-employed through markets, suppliers and associations. In the base case it costs ₹6.9 crore: ₹6.0 crore is the team and fixed costs, and ₹0.6 crore is winning the savers. At month 12 we read six numbers. If they sit at the base case, for example 22% still saving after a year and 9% of active savers borrowing, we stop. If they reach the best case, 36% and 23%, the model says LTV is 5.3 times CAC and we raise to scale. Cost per debit is settled before launch: a contract at ₹0.50 or less, or we switch to weekly debits. Likely question: Could a smaller pilot answer this? Answer: Sign-up and saving, yes. But loans and insurance only start at months 4 and 3, so cross-sell needs a year and enough savers who stay.
