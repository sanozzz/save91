# The proposition, and what bachatt.app said on 18 October 2026

The operator named this film as the proposition: [Bachatt app deta hai FD ke returns, ₹51 rupaye rozana se](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RgCWDsrnZNE). The pages below are what [bachatt.app](https://bachatt.app/) and [the about page](https://bachatt.app/about/about-bachatt) showed the same day. All of it is the company's own copy. None of it is a measured retention rate, a trail rate, or a loan book.

Spoken captions on the film did not come back from YouTube. What is kept is the title, the description, the length and the view count on the watch page.

This is the sentence the film sells. A daily amount, starting at ₹51, into a mutual fund that is said to beat a fixed deposit, with no lock-in, and the customer can see each step. The ₹300 in the illustration is the same daily amount the save91 base uses. The base collects it in cash through an agent. The film collects it in an app.

The homepage asks who Bachatt is for, and answers: salaried professionals, shop owners, jewellers, dealers, manufacturers, factory owners, and the people who work with them. The about page splits that into four:

## How the money moves

The page says Bachatt does not hold the money. It goes from the customer to the AMC or the bank. Bachatt is the platform.

The start is three steps on the phone:

1. Choose the SIP amount. The Insta Fund copy here is the 6 to 7% line.
2. Set up a UPI autopay. Pay ₹1 to set the mandate. Pause or cancel anytime.
3. A KYC of about two minutes: PAN, Aadhaar, Aadhaar OTP. The investment starts after KYC.

Support on the comparison table: WhatsApp and a call, 8am to 8pm. Withdrawal, on that table: instantly to the bank account.

## Loans, as the page lists the partners

Bachatt says it acts as a lending service provider. The loans are disbursed by RBI-registered NBFCs, and the partner decides eligibility.

## The same day, read again

These lines were on the site the same day and were not in the first write-up above.

The footer says Bachatt distributes regular plans. The commission the AMC pays Bachatt sits inside the scheme's expense ratio. Direct plans of the same schemes have a lower expense ratio. No percent is stated. That confirms a trail exists. It does not give the rate. The 0.35% in the save91 model stays a held number.

Support, on the customer-support page: a call and WhatsApp on +91 7982315462, 8 AM to 8 PM, all days. The email address on that page is hidden behind a script. The homepage had already said WhatsApp and a call across those hours.

The loans page names the product each logo is for. Experian, registered as Experian Credit Information Company of India Private Limited, is credit reports, scores and monitoring. L&T Finance Limited, Prefr, Moneyview and Kissht (Si Creva Capital Services Private Limited) are personal loans. Western Capital is unsecured business loans. FatakPay is registered as FDPL Finance Private Limited, with no product type on the card. Gromo and Moneyboxx have no product type on the card.

The FAQ answers "does Bachatt offer loans and credit cards" with personal and business loans and credit cards, in partnership with banks, NBFCs and fintechs. The partner decides approval, limits and pricing. That is a term loan the partner prices. It is not an overdraft against the amount saved, and it is not a home loan.

The safety FAQ says he can start from ₹100, the money goes to the AMC and not to the app, and the return is market-linked and not guaranteed. ₹100 on that page sits next to the film's ₹51 and next to the gold line, which is also from ₹100.

The SIP FAQ says the debit does not run on Saturday, Sunday or a market holiday. A daily amount in the film is not thirty calendar debits a month. The model's twenty deposit days stay for that reason. The FAQ does not state the number twenty.

One payment answer talks about a ₹2 charge, refunded in two or three days. The homepage still says ₹1 to set the mandate. Both lines are on the site.

A health-insurance terms page is linked from the footer. It is not a price and not a take-up.

## What this does to the case we have written

The film is a daily mutual-fund balance, from ₹51, said to beat an FD, with the money not sitting in the app. The site adds the loan and the card on top of that balance, and an AI layer that is not allowed, on its own disclaimer, to be personal advice. That is the same shape as the later notes: a small amount he is willing to save, and a borrow next to it. The site's loan line is "up to ₹5 lakh" and, on one screen, "up to ₹3 lakh". It is not an overdraft limit, and no rate is given.

The published base and the 18 Oct refresh are left as they were. This note does not put a trail rate, a retention rate, or an overdraft limit into either of them.
