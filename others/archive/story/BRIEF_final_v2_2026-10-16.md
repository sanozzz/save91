## The business, in one paragraph

This is an offline "save first, borrow later" business for India's self-employed daily earners (shopkeepers).

- A salaried street agent signs the shopkeeper up at his shop. He visits every 2–3 days to collect ₹350 a day (₹7,000 a month).
- The money goes into a fund in the saver's own name. The app never holds money.
- After 6 months of saving (a "proven saver"), we act as a Paisabazaar-like marketplace. We match him to the best loan from several lenders, and also offer credit cards, insurance and other products.
- We earn:
  - a fund commission of 0.35% a year on his balance;
  - a loan origination fee from the lender;
  - a fee per approved card;
  - commissions on insurance and other products;
  - a small fee on recovered bad loans.
- The question is whether this beats online acquisition (and Paytm) at winning and keeping this customer.
