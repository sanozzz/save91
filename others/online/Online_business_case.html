A saver won online costs ₹495 and returns ₹370 over five years in the main case: 0.75× LTV/CAC, paid back in month 75

0.75× in 5 years, 1.32× in 8LTV/CAC: what a saver brings in over 5 or 8 years, less the cost of serving them, divided by what it costs to win them (₹495). Undiscounted.
Cross-selling doesn't cover servingLoans ₹125 and cards ₹18 bring ₹143, less than the ₹190 of serving: 0.29× of the cost to win before serving, −0.10× after. Fund fees bring the rest.
Retention is the swingAt 30% still saving a year the saver returns 0.57× and does not pay back by year 8; at 40%, 0.98×, paid back in month 61.

WHAT'S UNCERTAIN

What's still uncertain is mostly how long savers keep saving: 0.60–0.93× at 5 years, 1.00–1.76× at 8 (8 in 10 draws)

Two inputs are still uncertainStill saving at 12 months (Bachatt's 30–40%) and the debt fund's fee (0.10–0.35%). Everything else is fixed by the founders: market figures, Bachatt, or our judgement.
Retention decides itAt 30% still saving the median is 0.57×; at 40%, 0.98×. The debt fund's fee moves it only from 0.72× to 0.79×. In 8 in 10 draws a saver pays back from month 64 to not by month 96.
The company, in every drawEBITDA is not positive by month 96 in any of the 1,000 draws. 8 in 10 draws need $8.8M+ to $9.8M+: the company is still losing money at month 96.
The Monte Carlo: we ran the model 1,000 times, picking retention and the debt fund's fee at random within their ranges. 0.3% of the 1,000 draws reach 1× in 5 years, holding everything else at the main case (cost to win ₹495). The main case is the median draw; 8 in 10 draws fall between the 10th and 90th percentiles. Undiscounted.

FOUR FACTS NOW, THEN THE SEED

Four facts we can ask for now decide most of the answer; the $0.5M seed then measures our own savers, loan funnel and cost to win

1Bachatt's average daily amountAsk Bachatt for the average, not the minimum. ₹100 is our product's floor; Bachatt's own minimums are lower (₹51/₹101 a day; ₹20 for its liquid fund).₹100: 0.75× · ₹130: 1.00× · ₹200: 1.59×Company: ₹200 a day gives profit from month 76 on $5.9M.
2Bachatt's exact share still saving at 12 monthsAsk Bachatt for its 12-month cohort curve; it has told us 30–40%.30%: 0.57× · 35%: 0.75× · 40%: 0.98×40.5% alone gives 1×; 45% (1.0% a month stopping after) needs $7.8M.
3Debits at ₹0 to us, in writingBSE StAR MF and the fund houses: a daily ₹100 debit on one mandate, at no cost to us.₹2 a weekly debit: 0.39× · ₹0: 0.75×Company: paying ₹2 a weekly debit raises the money needed to $11.7M+.
4The fee on the fund the ₹100 goes into, in writing0.64% blended or better: 80% equity at 0.75%, 20% debt at ~0.22%. Daily minimums are ~₹100 a scheme, so one hybrid fund must pay about 0.64% or more.0.5% on equity: 0.49× · 0.64%: 0.75×Company: at 0.5% on equity the money needed is $10.6M+.
