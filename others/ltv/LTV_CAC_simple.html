HOW ONE SAVER MAKES MONEY

At our targets, one online saver: ₹495 to win, ₹3,813 back in five years: 7.7×

Loans make the moneyLenders pay us a referral fee on every loan we bring: ₹3,686 of the ₹4,313 a saver brings in five years.
Saving pays its own way, justFund fees (₹531) about cover the cost of serving a saver (₹501): support, tech and payments.
7.7× is our target, not the main case₹3,813 back (revenue less serving) on ₹495 to win if every target holds. With the unmeasured inputs drawn, the median is 1.7× (page 5).

THE ODDS

With the cost to win fixed at ₹495, the median saver returns 1.7×; 23% of the what-ifs reach 3× and 16% do not pay back in five years

Cost to win is known₹600 in the seed and ₹450 after, measured on our own funnel, plus our 10% contingency. It is held fixed; only the value of a saver is drawn.
The value is notEach of 10 unmeasured inputs is equally likely anywhere between today's market and our target. Loan inputs move together: savers who want credit apply more, borrow more and are approved more.
7.7× is the top cornerIt needs every input at its target at the same time. None of the 1,000 what-ifs gets there; the best tenth reach 3.9× or more.
A Monte Carlo: 1,000 random what-ifs, the loan inputs moving together. Market values: the middle of the 2024–26 evidence. LTV/CAC: revenue less serving over 60 months, divided by the cost to win; undiscounted.

FIVE CHECKS

Five checks tell us whether the bet holds, most of them before we spend on growth

1
A lender's pre-screen
A lending partner soft-checks the credit of our first 1,000 KYC'd sign-ups.
Pass: approval near 30%, eligible loans near ₹3 lakh
2
Terms in writing
The payout, clawbacks and approval rules for savers with 3–6 months of saving, signed.
Pass: a payout of 2% or more after clawbacks
3
Who signs up
The income of the first sign-ups; it sets the loan size more than anything we assume.
Pass: income of ₹40k+ a month for ₹3 lakh loans
4
Opt-in and apply
How many KYC'd users ask to see lender offers, and how many apply in 3 months.
Pass: 35% or more opting in
5
The real cost to win
What the pilot pays per saver who makes a first debit.
Pass: ₹600 or less in the seed, ₹450 after
Checks 1 and 2 need no money lent and take weeks. If they pass, 7.7× has evidence behind it. If they come back at market levels, we know before the money goes into scale, and we change the plan: bigger savers, a cheaper way to win them, or more per loan.
Pass marks are the plan's own targets. A soft credit check reads a bureau file without touching the person's score.
