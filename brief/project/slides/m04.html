How sure can we be? I ran the shopkeeper business 10,000 times, drawing every uncertain input from its evidence range: what it costs to win a shopkeeper, how long they save, how many borrow, the rate, the loan size, losses, running costs and what we pay to borrow. A shopkeeper pays back in 64 in 100 of those futures, 51 in 100 within two years. The typical outcome is ₹226; the worst 5% lose more than −₹803, the best 5% earn more than ₹1,968. Worth pursuing, but not a sure thing.
