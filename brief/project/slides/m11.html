One pilot settles all four numbers. Win a first group of shopkeepers through one channel and count what each cost. Let them save for six months. Then lend to them jointly with a bank, so our capital at risk stays small, at a rate around 26%. Track how many borrow and how they repay for a year, against similar people who did not save. It passes only if all four clear their lines with room to spare: winning well under ₹704, borrowing well above 14%, a rate well above 24.4%, and losses well under 7.6% a year, ideally near 4%.
