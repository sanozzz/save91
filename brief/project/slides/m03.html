Why the money has to come from lending. A saver's fund fees earn us about ₹208 over five years. Passing one ₹1.5 lakh loan to another lender earns a referral fee of about ₹2,925. Lending it ourselves, after paying 13–15% for the money, normal bad loans and running costs, earns ₹4,594. So saving brings people in; lending ourselves pays the bills.
