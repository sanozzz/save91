A8

Salaried workers reached through employers or staffing firms like Quess stay longer: about 40% are still saving a year on, against about a quarter of shopkeepers. But they can usually borrow more cheaply elsewhere, so the rate we can charge decides it: across 10,000 futures it explains 46% of the outcome. At normal industry losses, a worker is worth −₹153 at 24% a year and ₹208 at 26%. If taking repayment straight from pay keeps losses low, it works at any rate shown: ₹748 at 24%. Overall they pay back in 43 in 100 futures at today's cost to win, and 65 in 100 if employers let us win them for ₹100. Your past lending found this route's market too small; the model says it works only if payroll deduction really lowers losses or the rate holds up.
