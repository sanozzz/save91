How big could it get? Winning 5 lakh shopkeepers a year, about about 1% of the 5.36 crore micro enterprises on Udyam, means lending about ₹1,749 crore a year on an average book of ₹985 crore. That creates ₹6 crore of value a year in a typical future and ₹38 crore if savers repay better, after the NBFC's own running costs. It needs ₹98 crore of capital if we co-lend with a bank, or ₹328 crore on our own books. A solid lending business rather than a giant one, unless the central belief holds.
