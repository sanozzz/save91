A12

Why did Bachatt get funded when our model says this business loses money? Two differences. First, it sells daily saving as mutual-fund SIPs through the exchange's fund platform, where the distributor pays nothing per transaction; the fund house pays the exchange ₹6–30. Our biggest cost, ₹5.90 on every debit, disappears. Second, savings is not where it makes money: it lends, up to ₹5 lakh, through partner banks and NBFCs. It claims 3M users, though its fund transactions suggest about 1 lakh save daily. Funding also reflects a bet on growth, not proven profits.
