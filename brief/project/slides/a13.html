Appendix · Model limits

Six things the lending model cannot tell you.

Saving's effect on repayment is assumed

The loss range spans better-than-industry to today's digital lenders; no data yet links saving to repayment.

More borrowing may mean riskier borrowers

Borrowing and losses are drawn independently; in reality they may move together.

The cost to win comes from consumer apps

No published figure exists for our channels; the pilot must measure it.

Rates may be pushed down

We assume 22–30% a year; competition for good shopkeepers could lower it.

The NBFC sits outside each customer

Its running cost and capital are shown separately, at three scales.

Five years only

Value beyond five years, and the cost of capital, are left out.
