Appendix A7

The hardest VC questions, with answers (1 of 3).

Why can't PhonePe or Jar just do this?

They could, and PhonePe already offers daily gold with Jar. Our only defensible edge would be credit: lending to savers better than a loan app, using the saving streak and payout data. The pilot must show it.

The base case loses money. Why fund anything?

Fund only the pilot: ₹6.9 crore for a year, with stop and go readings set in advance. Scale only if the three must-hold assumptions show up.

Why daily debits when each costs money?

The daily habit is the thesis. It works only at ₹0.50 a debit or less; the list price is ₹5. If we can't get that, we switch to weekly.

Isn't digital gold a regulatory time bomb?

It is a real risk: unregulated, with a framework under consultation. Plan B is ₹100+ fund debits. Gold can't back a loan in any case.

What stops savers leaving?

Nothing structural: any UPI app can now show and move a mandate. Retention is must-hold assumption 3; payday timing and goals are our levers.
