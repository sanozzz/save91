Where Save91 stands: strengths, gaps and rivals

Saving is a good way in, but it is not a moat. Save91 wins only if it reaches owners more cheaply than Bachatt and PhonePe, and is first to prove that savers repay.

A1 The answer in five lines

  • S

    Our best asset is a habit owners already have, kept in their own name. But only one of our five strengths is hard to copy: the founder's lending experience.

  • W

    At launch we are Bachatt's product, about 17 months later. An owner is worth +₹180 over five years if winning one costs ₹250, and −₹70 at today's evidence of about ₹500.

  • O

    6 in 10 small businesses have no formal loan, and online lenders are pulling back. No digital player has yet shown that a savings record earns an owner a loan.

  • T

    PhonePe has every piece but one, and 5 crore shops. Bachatt runs the same play with $16M and a head start.

  • So

    Compete on how we win owners and on what we know about their money, not on the savings product. Prove the three numbers within 12 months, with a stop rule agreed now.

A3 What the SWOT tells us to do

Four moves. Each one uses a strength or an opening to answer a weakness or a threat.

M1

Win on the channel, not on the product

The problem
Our savings product is easy to copy: PhonePe starts at ₹10 a day and Bachatt already pays agents.
The move
Win owners where they already trust someone: trade associations, wholesale markets and suppliers' sales staff. Pay agents for owners still saving after 3 months, not for sign-ups.
How we will know
Cost per owner still saving at month 3 is ₹250 or less. Today's evidence is about ₹500.

Built from S1W2W5T2

M2

Make saving fit uneven income: save a slice of the day's takings

The problem
A fixed ₹100 a day fails on slow days. Daily debits fail often, and a third of small daily SIPs stop within a year.
The move
Let the daily amount follow the money coming in, for example 2% of yesterday's credits up to a cap. Nobody offers this yet. It needs bank data through a regulated partner, and a lawyer's check on varying amounts under the 24-hour notice rule.
How we will know
Share still saving at month 12, against a test group on a fixed ₹100.

Built from S3O4W4T4

M3

Sell lenders a better borrower, not a lead

The problem
Referral fees are thin, lenders are pulling back, and partner loans are 80% of our revenue.
The move
Give each lender a borrower with a saving streak and, with consent, a year of whole-account cash flow. Ask for a higher fee for better loans. Offer first-loss cover, up to the 5% cap, only on loans we pick.
How we will know
Savers' approval rate, and the share 90+ days late, against each lender's normal book.

Built from S3O1O2O5W3T3

M4

Prove three numbers in 12 months, then decide on our own book

The problem
The plan pays at ₹250 to win and 1 in 5 borrowing. At today's evidence it does not.
The move
Run the pilot on the three pass marks. Start the NBFC application only if losses look like 7.5% a year or less: it takes 8–14 months and ₹10 crore of capital.
How we will know
A stop rule to agree now: if a saver still costs over ₹500 to win and fewer than 1 in 10 borrow after 12 months, stop or change the model. At those numbers the model says each owner loses about ₹280.

Built from W2W6O7T5

A4 The competitive landscape

Every player ends at loans. They differ in how they reach the owner and who collects the repayment.

Payment apps that lend to shops

High threat

PhonePe, Paytm, BharatPe, Google Pay

How they reach owners
A free QR code and speaker at the counter
How they make money
Shop loans repaid from daily sales. PhonePe arranges them with 56 lenders; BharatPe also lends from its own NBFC

PhonePe arranged ₹4,507 cr of shop loans in FY25 and earned ₹328 cr on them. Paytm has 1.57 cr shops paying for a device (Jun 2026)

Where they beat us

They see every sale on their QR and take the instalment out of it. Huge reach and field staff.

Where we beat them

Each sees one QR of about 9. Only about 1 in 4 registered PhonePe shops use it in a month. Savings is a side line: Paytm calls its fund and gold revenue 'negligible'.

Business Today (Mar 2026); Paytm Q1 FY27 release · 2026-07

Daily-saving app for business owners

High threat

Bachatt

How they reach owners
Daily auto-debit into debt funds from ₹100; paid agents (Bachatt Partner) sign up savers
How they make money
Fund commission, plus referral fees on loans up to ₹5 lakh from 7 lenders and on credit cards

$16M raised (Accel, Lightspeed, Info Edge). Claims 3M users. Has published no retention, loan or default figure

Where they beat us

About 17 months and $15M ahead, with an agent network, on the same customer.

Where we beat them

Refers loans only, and has published none of the three numbers.

Bachatt Series A release (Mar 2026) · 2026-03

Consumer saving apps

Medium threat

PhonePe Daily SIP, Jar, Gullak, Paytm Money

How they reach owners
Daily saving from ₹10 (funds or digital gold) inside a consumer app
How they make money
Gold margin (Jar), fund commission, then insurance and loans

PhonePe Daily SIP: 5 lakh+ investors in 8 months, 77% from tier-2 and tier-3 towns. Jar: 35M registered users, ₹208 cr operating revenue in FY25 (its own definition)

Where they beat us

Cheaper to start (₹10), bigger brands, already on the owner's phone.

Where we beat them

Built for young consumers, not for a shop's money needs: no business loans or shop cover around the saving.

Outlook Business (3 Aug 2026); Entrackr (Jar FY25) · 2026-08

The cash agent and the credit co-op

Medium threat

Daily-deposit (pigmy) agents, credit co-op societies, chit funds, bank daily-deposit schemes

How they reach owners
An agent comes to the counter every day and takes cash
How they make money
Lending margin on loans to members; the agent earns a commission

Maharashtra alone: 2 lakh+ daily agents serving about 1 crore families; about 2 lakh credit co-ops nationally

Where they beat us

Cash, face to face, and a loan from the same society. This is the habit we must replace.

Where we beat them

Low interest, a penalty for taking money out early, and no deposit insurance. Sahara repaid 27 lakh of 1.35 crore depositors by Jul 2025.

Indian Cooperative (2025–26) · 2025-26

Online business lenders

Low threat

Lendingkart, FlexiLoans, NeoGrowth, Indifi

How they reach owners
Online ads and loan agents (DSAs)
How they make money
Interest on their own loan book

Lendingkart's loan book fell 74% and revenue 62% to ₹327 cr in FY26; NeoGrowth's book fell about 21%

Where they beat us

They lend from their own money and set their own terms.

Where we beat them

They are retreating after bad loans rose, and have no daily contact with the owner. They could become our lenders.

StartupFeed (5 Oct 2026) · 2026-10

Gold-loan lenders

Medium threat

Muthoot, Manappuram, banks, Rupeek

How they reach owners
Branches in every market; no income proof needed
How they make money
Interest on loans secured by gold

₹19.4 lakh crore gold-loan book (Mar 2026), up from ₹6.3 lakh crore in 2023; average loan ₹1.96 lakh

Where they beat us

Fast, cheap, secured and familiar.

Where we beat them

They need gold and a branch visit, and do nothing for saving. Better as a partner product than a rival.

Experian India via Moneylife (Jun 2026) · 2026-06

Business account and bookkeeping apps

Medium threat

Tide India, Khatabook, OkCredit, Vyapar

How they reach owners
A business bank account, invoicing or a credit ledger the owner opens every day
How they make money
Fees, subscriptions and loan referrals (Tide works with about 25 lenders)

Tide: 800,000+ Indian businesses by Sep 2025, over half of its 1.6M members worldwide

Where they beat us

They see the business's books and money too. Tide also offers fixed deposits.

Where we beat them

None runs daily saving; they serve the business, we serve the owner's own money. We found no reliable recent figures for Khatabook.

TechCrunch (21 Sep 2025) · 2025-09

Supplier credit and gig platforms (side slices)

Low threat

udaanCapital and FMCG ordering apps; Swiggy, Urban Company

How they reach owners
Credit at the moment the shop buys stock; loans and saving inside a gig app
How they make money
Supply-chain finance and referral fees

udaanCapital: ₹8,000 cr a year to 1.5 lakh kiranas (2022). Swiggy rider loans ₹102 cr (2023); Swiggy × Zerodha saving from ₹100 (Jun 2026)

Where they beat us

Credit exactly when it is needed, with repayment built into the next order or payout.

Where we beat them

Narrow: one supplier's stock or one platform's payout.

A6 The evidence, item by item

Strengths: the evidence, item by item
S1

We ride a habit that already exists

Owners already hand cash to an agent every day. We move that habit to an app; we do not have to create it.

Indian Cooperative, as used in your deck (A8) · 2025-26

S2

The money is safe and stays in the owner's own name

A regulated fund, held in the owner's name, that they can take out any day. A credit society can fail: Sahara's four societies had repaid 27 lakh of their 1.35 crore depositors by Jul 2025.

Angel One on Sahara refunds (Jul 2025); SEBI mutual fund master circular (Mar 2026) · 2025-07

S3

We can see all of the owner's money, not one QR's slice

A shop holds about 9 QR codes, so each QR lender sees one slice. All of it lands in the owner's own bank account. With the owner's consent, a lender can read every inflow through the Account Aggregator system. Reading it needs a regulated lender partner, so this is an edge only once we build our credit checks on it.

PIB (Dec 2025), as used in your deck (A25); Sahamati (Sep 2026) · 2025-12

S4

Nothing of ours is at risk at the start

Partner lenders book the loans and the fund house holds the money, so the pilot needs no lending licence and no loan book. Daily fund saving on the exchange has no per-debit fee for us, because the fund house pays it.

Cafemutual on BSE StAR MF (row XB-01 in your research) · 2016-18

S5

A founder who has run a lending business

Credit checks, collections and lender terms decide whether this model works. The founder has run all three before. Experience like that takes years to copy.

Your pitch deck, slide 9 · 2026-10

Weaknesses: the evidence, item by item
W1

Saving earns us almost nothing

The fund pays us 0.2–0.5% of the balance a year. Over five years savings is ₹31 of the ₹542 an owner brings in. Every other product has to pay for the saver.

Kotak MF on trail; your model (tables/redesign_deck.csv) · 2026-10

W2

Everything hangs on one number we have not measured

If winning an owner costs ₹250, each is worth +₹180 over five years. At today's evidence of about ₹500, the same owner is worth −₹70. Both assume 1 in 5 borrow each year.

Your model (tables/redesign_grid.csv) · 2026-10

W3

Referring a loan earns a fraction of lending it

On a ₹2 lakh loan we would earn ₹2,000–5,000, once. PhonePe earned about ₹14,600 on the same amount, because it also collects. Partner loans are about 80% of our revenue, so lenders' appetite decides our income.

GroMo agent payouts (Aug 2026); Business Today on PhonePe (Mar 2026) · 2026-08

W4

We have no grip on repayment, and we cannot take cash

QR lenders cut the instalment from the shop's daily sales before paying it out. We would collect by bank mandate, and daily debits fail often. We also cannot do the agent's best trick: collect cash at the counter.

Mint on NPCI data (Sep 2025) · 2025-08

W5

We start late, small, and with the same product

Bachatt launched in May 2025 and has raised $16M. PhonePe's Daily SIP starts at ₹10 a day; our plan says ₹100. Same funds, same UPI AutoPay.

Bachatt Series A release (Mar 2026); Angel One on PhonePe Daily SIP (Dec 2025) · 2026-03

W6

Growth eats cash before it pays

In year one an owner brings in +₹119 but costs ₹250 to win: −₹131 at month 12, paid back by month 21. Each extra owner won means more cash out first. The team is still to be hired.

Your model (tables/redesign_year1.csv); pitch deck slide 9 · 2026-10

Opportunities: the evidence, item by item
O1

Only about 4 in 10 small businesses have formal credit

41% of small-business borrowers had an active formal loan in March 2026. Borrowing is the biggest gap in their money life, and it is the product that pays.

TransUnion CIBIL-SIDBI MSME Pulse (Jul 2026) · 2026-07

O2

Online business lenders are pulling back

Lendingkart cut its loan book 74% in FY26 and NeoGrowth about 21%, after bad loans rose. Small-business credit overall still grew 12.8%. Owners are being turned away, and lenders want borrowers who are already screened.

StartupFeed (5 Oct 2026), citing filings and CRIF High Mark · 2026-10

O3

No digital player has shown that saving earns owners a loan

'Save with us, borrow from us' works at credit co-op societies, but they are uninsured and some fail. Bachatt says it uses saving behaviour to lend, but has published no loan numbers. The first to prove it owns the story.

PIB Ministry of Cooperation (2025); Bachatt site; your deck (A16) · 2025

O4

Nobody saves a slice of the day's sales yet

We found no product at Paytm, PhonePe, BharatPe or Pine Labs that saves part of a shop's daily takings. An amount that follows sales fits uneven income better than a fixed ₹100.

Your research (row NG-046); deck A45 · 2026-09

O5

Lending on the whole bank account is going mainstream

Bank-data sharing with consent (Account Aggregator) backed ₹3.82 lakh crore of loans in FY26: 8.4% of retail and small-business lending by value. The pipes to lend on all of an owner's inflows exist now.

Sahamati (2 Sep 2026) · 2026-09

O6

Owners want fast, secured credit: gold loans tripled in three years

Gold loans grew from ₹6.3 to ₹19.4 lakh crore (Mar 2023 to Mar 2026). The average loan is ₹1.96 lakh and 3 in 4 go to repeat borrowers, more and more for shop working capital. Gold-loan and loan-against-fund partners are an easy first loan to sell.

Experian India via Moneylife (Jun 2026) · 2026-06

O7

Later: lending ourselves turns a loss into a profit

Referring loans, an owner is worth −₹54 over five years at today's ₹499 to win. Lending ourselves: +₹205 at normal losses, +₹859 if savers repay better. RBI's co-lending rules (Jan 2026) need us to keep only 10% of each loan.

Your model (tables/lend_customer.csv); RBI co-lending rules via Business Standard · 2026-10

Threats: the evidence, item by item
T1

PhonePe can join the dots

It has 5 crore+ registered shops, a Daily SIP from ₹10 with 5 lakh+ investors, loans against mutual funds, and ₹4,507 crore of shop loans (FY25) repaid from daily sales. The one piece it lacks, saving from sales, is a feature it could ship.

PhonePe press (Apr 2026); Outlook Business (3 Aug 2026); Business Today (Mar 2026) · 2026-08

T2

Bachatt is about 17 months ahead on the same play

Daily saving for shop owners, loans up to ₹5 lakh through 7 lenders, paid agents who sign up savers, and $16M raised with Accel leading. It claims 3M users.

Bachatt Series A release (Mar 2026); Entrackr · 2026-03

T3

Lenders are getting stricter with small businesses

The same pullback as O2 means partner lenders approve fewer loans and push fees down. RBI caps any first-loss cover we give a lender at 5% of the loans.

Crisil Ratings (Nov 2025) via StartupFeed; RBI Digital Lending Directions (May 2025) · 2025-05

T4

Savers stop, debits fail, and leaving takes one tap

Over a third (35.6%) of SBI's ₹250 daily SIPs opened in FY26 had stopped by April 2026. Since Dec 2025 any UPI app can show a person's AutoPay mandates and move them.

Business Standard citing SBI Funds' RHP (Jul 2026); NPCI OC-223 · 2026-07

T5

New rules can shrink what each product earns

IRDAI's draft caps insurance commissions (15% on a first-year health policy, less on insurance sold with a loan); comments close 25 Oct 2026. No cashback or gifts on fund saving. An NBFC of our own needs ₹10 crore of its own capital.

IRDAI consultation (23 Sep 2026) via Khaitan & Co; AMFI code of conduct; RBI · 2026-09

T6

Owners already have fast credit elsewhere

Gold loans need no income proof, and from Apr 2026 lend up to 85% of the gold's value on loans under ₹2.5 lakh. Payment apps pre-approve shop loans from sales data. Our loan has to be better than these, not just available.

RBI gold and silver loan directions (Jun 2025), as reported Apr-May 2026 · 2026-04
