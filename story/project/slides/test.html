Say: The next step is not a big round; it is a one-year pilot with 25,000 savers in Delhi NCR, half gig workers, half the self-employed. It costs about ₹6.9 crore in the middle case, mostly the team. At month 12 we read the three numbers on this slide. If they sit at the middle case, we stop. If they reach the best case, the model says each saver earns 5.3 times what it cost to win them, and we raise to scale. The debit price is settled before launch, in a signed contract, so the first question is answered before a rupee of sign-up money is spent. Likely question: Could a smaller or shorter pilot do? Answer: Sign-ups and saving, yes. But loans start in month 4 and insurance in month 3, so the third question needs a year and enough savers who stay.
