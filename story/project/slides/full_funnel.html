Why lending-first leaks

Lending-first pays to find four applicants for every borrower, and covering all of the lender's losses is now barred.

You pay to acquire every applicant, then turn most away. Savings-first turns nobody away: anyone can save ₹10.

Say: This is the leak in lending-first. Of 1,000 applicants, a lend-first app approved 250, and 234 repaid the first loan without going 90+ days late. Each applicant cost ₹190 to find, check and KYC, so each borrower cost ₹760 and each good borrower ₹813. The second problem is structural. Lend-first apps did it by covering 100% of the partner NBFC's losses. Under RBI's digital lending rules any guarantee on a loan book counts as default-loss cover, and an app may cover at most 5%. So that model can't run today without our own NBFC, which needs ₹10 crore minimum. Likely question: Couldn't a better model fix the approval rate? Answer: Partly. But the people you reject still cost money to find, and the capital problem remains.
