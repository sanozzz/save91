Appendix A8

The hardest VC questions, with answers (2 of 3).

Do savers really make better borrowers?

Not proven in India. Savings-linked group loans show NPA of 2.05% vs 8.72% for microfinance, but that is correlation. The pilot measures late loans against the 6-7% benchmark.

Why not lend from your own NBFC?

It needs ₹10 crore and equity near a third of the book, and small NBFCs lose money for years. Tested as lever B: best case 3.6 against 5.3 for the plan.

What's the moat?

Weak, honestly. Candidates: platform partnerships, a marketplace where lenders compete for pre-qualified savers, and referral loops without cash bonuses. None is proven.

How big can this get?

Best case $115M in year 10 from 7.3M active savers. Gig workers alone stop at $93M.

What kind of company is this?

A distributor and software company, not a lender: fund distributor, insurance agent, gold distributor and loan service provider with a 5% loss cover. Thin margins; valued like distribution.
