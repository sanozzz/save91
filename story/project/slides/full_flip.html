The flip

The flip: everyone can save, so start there and offer credit once the habit and the data exist.

Nobody is turned away at the front door. Each step up uses what the earlier steps showed us: the saving streak, the payout record and the FD.

Say: This is the flip. Instead of lending first and rejecting most people, everyone starts by saving, from ₹10 a day into gold and a liquid fund. Insurance comes from month 3. A first small loan comes from month 4: a partner lender funds it, and we cover up to 5% of its losses. From month 6 the saver can move goal money into a bank FD, get a card backed by it, and start an equity SIP. An unsecured card follows from month 18 for good savers. Every product is sold by a regulated partner; we distribute and never hold the money. Likely question: Why would a saver want a loan at all? Answer: They already borrow, from loan apps and moneylenders. We offer a cheaper loan to people whose saving we can see.
