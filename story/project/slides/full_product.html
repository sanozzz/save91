Say: Follow the money. The platform pays the rider's own bank account. A UPI Autopay mandate, a standing instruction the rider sets once, debits a small amount every day and sends it straight to three partners. The fund house and the bank are regulated; the gold vault partner is not, because nobody regulates digital gold. Digital gold, held in a partner's vault, takes ₹10, so it carries the daily habit. A liquid fund, a very short-term debt fund from a fund house, takes ₹100 a debit and lets the rider take out up to ₹50,000 a day instantly. A bank FD holds goal money for 7 days or more and can back a secured card. Our app is only the window: goals, streaks and offers. It never touches the money, because holding it would be an unregulated deposit, and fund purchases must come from the investor's own account. Likely question: Can the platform deduct savings straight from the payout? Answer: Not into a fund. The payout has to land in the worker's bank first; the worker's own mandate then debits it.
