# The customer's problem

This is the page the deck does not have. The published deck opens on our cost of winning him online (₹499, and 24 in 100 still saving). That is our problem. His is below. The words are the ones to put on the page, before Part 1.

The proposition, as Bachatt states it, is in `story/BACHATT_PROPOSITION.md`. The film the operator named is an 18-second advertisement: FD-like returns from ₹51 a day, no lock-in, withdraw whenever, ICICI and SBI funds, an SMS at each step. The illustration in that film is ₹300 a day. The site read the same day adds a loan of up to ₹5 lakh and a card, and says the app does not hold the money.

## What is wrong for him

1. **He is sold on "believe me".** An investment person he knows picks the product. Nothing is written down. He cannot see the rule that chose it.
2. **He cannot start a small SIP himself.** A small instalment happens today because a person comes to the shop and collects it.
3. **Once the money is in, he is forgotten.** Nobody comes back to ask what else he needs.
4. **He saves a small amount, and then many stop.** He has a small amount he is happy to save. Some keep going. The rest drop off. That drop-off is the problem Aniket named. He gave no amount and no share.

## What the pilot has to show

- That a saver can be told, in one sentence, why this product was chosen for him.
- Whether the small instalment is a UPI mandate he starts, or cash a person collects. Not both as the base.
- That someone comes back after the first month for a reason other than taking the instalment, and what that visit costs. If the reason is the loan, month-12 survival has to be split by whether the customer got the loan they asked for.
- The amount he is still saving after the first month, and the share who dropped off. Aniket said the amount is small. He did not say how small.

## What he thinks the product is

The operator's reading, PR-05. He thinks of Bachatt as a digital wallet. He saves something, and then he borrows. The product he is reaching for is an OD++. No limit and no rate were given.

The base is not that product. The balance is a debt fund in his name. The borrow is a term loan. The right to borrow is not tied to the small amount he saved. An app wallet that held the money sits on the folder's own rule that the app must not hold his money.
