Anyone can sign up savers; a product built for how they save and borrow is still missing, and the market is big enough for more than one winner

1
Sold on “believe me”
  • ✕An investment person they know picks the product. Nothing is written down.
  • ✓A written rule they can see: why this fund, in one sentence.
2
Can't start small alone
  • ✕A small instalment happens today because someone comes to collect it.
  • ✓₹100 a day by UPI Autopay, set up in minutes; an agent for 1 in 10.
3
Forgotten after joining
  • ✕Nobody comes back to ask what else the saver needs.
  • ✓Streaks, nudges and an AI money coach in the app.
4
Saves a little, then stops
  • ✕Some keep saving; many drop off.
  • ✓Saving improves the offer: the longer the streak, the better the loan limit and rate (our proposal).
What savers think the product is: a wallet they save into and then borrow from, an overdraft-like line. Our answer keeps the money in funds in the saver's own name (we never hold it) and lets saving improve the loan offer.
