THE SAVER'S PROBLEM, NOT OURS

They will save a small amount every day and want to borrow when they need to; nobody serves both

1
Sold on “believe me”
  • ✕An investment person they know picks the product. Nothing is written down.
  • ✓A written rule they can see: why this fund, in one sentence.
2
Can't start small alone
  • ✕A small instalment happens today because someone comes to collect it.
  • ✓₹100 a day by UPI Autopay, set up in minutes; an agent for 1 in 10.
3
Forgotten after joining
  • ✕Nobody comes back to ask what else the saver needs.
  • ✓Streaks, nudges and an AI money coach in the app.
4
Saves a little, then stops
  • ✕Some keep saving; many drop off.
  • ✓Credit tied to the streak: save 6 months, see your loan offer (our proposal).
What savers think the product is: a wallet they save into and then borrow from, an overdraft-like line. Our answer keeps the money in funds in the saver's own name (we never hold it) and makes credit the reward for saving.
Sources: practitioner conversations, 17–18 Oct 2026 (customer problem notes; the wallet reading is the founders' own).
