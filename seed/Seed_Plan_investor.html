Save91 / SaveMore · working nameHow it works
THE MONEY FLOW

Money moves from the saver's bank to the fund and back, never through us

In the saver's nameUnits sit with the fund house, registered to the saver.
Never through usWe hold no customer money, ever.
Paid by partnersFund houses and lenders pay us. The fund house pays out of its published fee: a regular plan costs the saver about 0.5–1% a year more than a direct plan, and we say so up front.
Sources: SEBI (payments come from the investor's own account), BSE StAR MF (no transaction fee for distributors), fund-house disclosures (distributor trail).

Data protectionconsent flows and data kept in India
After launch · months 4–12
Loans as a DSA / lending service providerfrom month 7: partner lenders pay us per loan; RBI digital-lending rules apply (the borrower pays us nothing)
Insurance through a broker partnerfrom month 7, for a fixed referral fee; our own registration later
Credit cards with issuersthe issuer decides every card
Legal opinions firstlender-offer consent, the insurance fee, agent rules; agents never handle cash

✕ Run lucky draws or savings circles

Sources: AMFI, NISM, RBI Digital Lending Directions 2025, RBI NBFC capital norms, IRDAI, Banning of Unregulated Deposit Schemes Act 2019, Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023.
